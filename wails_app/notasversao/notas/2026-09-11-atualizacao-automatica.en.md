# Automatic updates and what's new

## New features
- Hanzi Tracker now updates itself when it opens, through the Stable and Dev channels.
- While updating, a screen shows the download and installation progress of the new version.
- In Settings → Info, you can choose the update channel and use the "Check now" button.
- After each update, a pop-up shows what's new and what was fixed in the new version.
- The "What's New" button in Settings → Info reopens the full change history at any time.

## Fixes
- The engine and language preferences chosen in the installer are now applied on the app's first launch.
